Persona
Name: Priya Sharma
Role: Urban professional, product manager, 27, lives alone in Pune.
Archetype: A busy, tech-comfortable person who generates repetitive life-admin tasks (grocery restocking, expense tracking) but lacks a unified system to manage them. She is not an early adopter — she uses technology when it removes friction, not for its own sake.
Core need: Reduce the mental overhead of recurring decisions without losing control over what actually gets executed.
Key behavioural insight: Priya trusts AI suggestions but will not accept autonomous action. She wants a draft to approve, not a decision already made. This directly justifies the PENDING_APPROVAL pattern and the AI Suggestion card being the first thing she sees on the home screen.
Pain points that the app addresses: Forgetting to restock basics, no single view of household spend, managing grocery orders across multiple apps.
Design implications:

The AI suggestion card must always lead — it is the primary value delivery point.
Approve/Dismiss must be one tap, never buried.
Planned modules must be visible but non-intrusive — she should see the roadmap without feeling the app is incomplete.
Language should be plain and conversational, not technical. ("Looks like you're running low" not "Inventory threshold alert".)
