Priya Sharma

"I want my life to run itself so I can focus on what actually matters."
Pune, India
Product manager, 27
Lives alone
Tech-comfortable
Day-to-day reality
Long work hours
Orders groceries weekly
Tracks expenses loosely
Forgets to restock basics
Relies on DMart / Blinkit
Goals
Never run out of essentials
Spend within a monthly budget
Reduce decision fatigue
One place for all life data
Pain points
Reruns the same grocery list mentally
No visibility on monthly spend
Too many apps, no single view
Hates surprise "out of stock" moments
Relationship with AI
Trusts suggestions, not blind actions
Wants to approve before anything executes
Values explainability
Already uses ChatGPT casually
In her own words
"I don't need the app to think for me — I just need it to do the boring thinking so I can say yes or no. Give me the draft, I'll approve it."
— Priya, on the human-in-the-loop approval model
